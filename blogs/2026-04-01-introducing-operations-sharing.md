The Problem: Fragmented Regional Coordination In shared airspace, operational efficiency and safety hinge on cross-organization coordination. But when multiple entities are flying in the same area, there’s often no visibility into each other’s planned operations. Without a shared coordination layer, crews are showing up in the field with little to no awareness of nearby activity already underway — making regional alignment a constant challenge.
